What we gain

The shortest journey in the whole document 4h 25m out and 4h 30m back, direct, no layover. For every other destination the long leg is over 12 hours and has at least one stop.
Only two bookings and no hotel nights Wizz and Air Arabia, that is all. Both connections are handled within the same day, so we pay for no accommodation en route — but the outbound night is lost anyway.

Worth knowing

  • It has different dates from the rest. We leave on Sunday the 14th and get home on Thursday 25 February, not Sunday the 28th. That works out at 9 nights in the Maldives, the shortest stay in the document.
  • The Dubai → Sharjah transfer is at night. We land at 20:20 and Air Arabia leaves at 02:45 — those 6h 25m go on the 25 km and on waiting, even though we pay for no hotel.
  • The return is more expensive than the outbound with Air Arabia: AED 1,021 per person against AED 785.
  • The Air Arabia and Wizz fares are Basic — checked baggage is paid separately on all four flights and is not in the prices above.
  • On the way back the transfer is in daylight, with 6h 20m available, so that part is relaxed.
