How we cut the cost

Kiwi Basic, not Guarantee €556 / person against €622 with Kiwi.com Guarantee. −€66 per person, −€264 in total · but Basic covers us for nothing if a flight is cancelled, and we do the check-in manually.
There is no better day for the London flight €44 on Thursday the 25th and Sunday the 28th, against €54 on Saturday the 27th. But the 25th is before we get there, and the 28th would mean a night in London — far more than the €40 saved.

Worth knowing

  • Three separate bookings — Ryanair to Vienna, Kiwi for Vienna → Ho Chi Minh → London, and Wizz from Luton home. If one falls through, the others are not rebooked.
  • The 09:30 Wizz flight from Luton is impossible — we only land at Gatwick at 13:40. The table uses the 18:55 service, at the same price.
  • The departure from Ho Chi Minh is at 04:10, so the last night goes on the trip to the airport.
  • Scoot is low-cost: the price covers one cabin bag, checked baggage is paid separately.
  • 12 nights in Ho Chi Minh, from Monday the 15th to Saturday 27 February.

What we gain

A whole extra day in Vietnam We leave on Saturday 27 February at 19:55, not at 04:10 as in option A. Saturday stays a full day in Ho Chi Minh instead of going on the trip to the airport in the middle of the night.
A single airline on the long leg Singapore Airlines takes us there and back, with the Singapore layover on the same ticket. If we are delayed, they rebook us — unlike A, where there are three separate bookings.

Worth knowing

  • It is €201 more expensive than option A, but it has a single night of accommodation, in Dubai, against one in Vienna — so the real difference is smaller.
  • The return layover is 15h 35m in Singapore, in the airport, between 09:05 and 14:30 the next day. Outbound it is 6h 40m.
  • The risk is in Dubai, on the way back: 3h 05m between the Singapore Airlines arrival at Terminal 1 and the Wizz flight from Terminal 2, on separate tickets. Exactly the Manila problem.
  • We get home on Monday 1 March at 00:40, after midnight.
  • The Singapore Airlines fare is Economy Lite — to be checked what it includes for baggage.
