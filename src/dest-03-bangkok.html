Worth knowing

  • The €45 flight from Charleroi cannot be taken. It leaves at 06:40, and we land at Brussels-Zaventem at 05:45, a different airport. The table uses the €48 price, for the 14:50 service.

How we cut the cost

We come back on the 27th, via Larnaca Instead of Wizz direct Dubai → Bucharest on the 28th, at €156 / person, we take Wizz from Abu Dhabi to Larnaca on the 27th and on home on the 28th: €102 / person, that is −€217 in total. It does require moving the Air Arabia flight to the 27th, a Sharjah → Abu Dhabi transfer of ≈150 km and a night in Larnaca.
No hotel nights en route Both connections are handled within the same day. On the way out the night goes on the transfer to Sharjah, but we pay for no accommodation, out or back.

Worth knowing

  • The longest stay in the document: 14 nights in Bangkok, from Sunday the 14th to Sunday 28 February. A has 12, B has 13.
  • A direct flight on the long leg, 6h 15m out and 7h back. No connection to catch.
  • The outbound transfer is at night and it is tight: we land in Dubai at 20:20 and Air Arabia leaves Sharjah at 01:10 — 4h 50m for 25 km plus check-in.
  • The Air Arabia and Wizz fares are Basic — checked baggage is paid separately on every flight and is not in the prices above.
  • We get home on Monday 1 March at 00:40, after midnight.
