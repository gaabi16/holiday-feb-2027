How we cut the cost

Gulf Air: through a third-party site, not the official one Gotogate asks €534 / person against €559 / person on gulfair.com. −€25 per person, −€100 in total · the Gotogate fare also includes checked baggage.
Aegean: BudgetAir is the cheapest, but poorly rated €147 / person at BudgetAir (3.6 stars) against €160 / person at Trip.com (4.8 stars). −€53 in total · neither includes checked baggage.

Worth knowing

  • 13 full days in Malaysia. We arrive Sunday 14 Feb at 15:30 and leave Saturday 27 Feb at 17:35.
  • Checked baggage is missing from the Aegean ticket — the fares we found are cabin-bag only; with Gulf Air it is included. The cost comes on top of the prices above.
  • The three Gulf Air flights are on the same ticket. If we miss a connection in Bahrain or Singapore, they rebook us. The risk is only in Athens, where the ticket is separate.
  • In Singapore we do not get off with the bags — it is the same flight, GF165, carrying on to Kuala Lumpur.
  • Saturday the 13th goes almost entirely on travel: we leave at 10:15 and only fly out of Athens at 14:40.
