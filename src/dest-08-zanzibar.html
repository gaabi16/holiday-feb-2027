How we cut the cost

Ethiopian through a third-party site, not the official one €353 / person at lastminute.com against AED 6,600 on ethiopianairlines.com, which is €387 / person. −€34 per person, −€137 in total · the lastminute fare also includes checked baggage.
Only two bookings Wizz and Ethiopian, that is all. Both Addis Ababa layovers are inside the Ethiopian booking, so there they rebook us if we are delayed.

Worth knowing

  • The 35-minute layover in Addis Ababa is the tightest in the whole document. It is protected by the Ethiopian booking, but if we miss it we lose a whole day of the holiday.
  • 18 hours in Abu Dhabi on the way back. We land Saturday at 03:10 and Wizz leaves at 21:15. Effectively a day to pay for or to spend in the airport.
  • We leave from Cluj-Napoca, as in Manila option C, so the journey there is added on — ≈450 km from Bucharest.
  • Checked baggage included on the long leg, in the lastminute.com fare. With Wizz it is not included.
  • The journey is short for how far we get: 7h 30m outbound. Only the Maldives is quicker.
  • 12 nights in Zanzibar, from Sunday the 14th to Friday 26 February.
