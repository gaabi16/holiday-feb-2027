Worth knowing

  • The long flight leaves Monday the 15th at 19:50, so we come out with Wizz on Sunday the 14th and sleep a night in Dubai. If we came on Saturday, it would be two nights.
  • 17 hours in Singapore on the way back, between Friday 21:30 and Saturday 14:30. That is a whole night, in the airport or in a hotel.
  • The Singapore ↔ Da Nang leg is operated by Scoot, Singapore Airlines' low-cost arm. The fare conditions stay the same, baggage included, but there is no in-flight entertainment and seat selection is paid for.
  • The Gulf transfer is as tight as in Ho Chi Minh C: 3 hours for 130 km, Dubai → Abu Dhabi.
  • 10 nights in Da Nang, from Tuesday the 16th to Friday 26 February — the shortest stay of all the destinations.
