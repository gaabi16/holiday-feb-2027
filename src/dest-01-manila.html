The recurring problem: separate tickets

In options A and B, Wizz Air operates from Terminal 2 in Dubai, while Singapore Airlines and Kuwait Airways are both in Terminal 1. The two terminals are not connected airside — T2 is on the other side of the airport, and the transfer is by road, 15–20 minutes by taxi.

Because these are separate tickets, at every change we have to reclaim the bags, clear passport control, move by taxi and check in again. No airline rebooks us if we miss the flight.

Option A only has the problem on the way back: we land at 18:00, Wizz leaves at 21:05. Of those 3h 05m, realistically about two hours remain before check-in closes. Doable, but with no margin for error.

Option B has the problem on the way out, where it is worse: we land at 20:20, Kuwait leaves at 22:55. Since check-in closes an hour before, that leaves under an hour and a half for bags, passports, taxi and the desk.

Option D has the same problem, but in Rome and with more margin: 3h 20m out and 3h 45m back. Which terminals Wizz and Gulf Air use at Fiumicino is still to be checked.

In short: B gives us more time in the Philippines, but moves the risk to the start of the holiday, where a missed connection ruins everything after it. It is only worth it if we find an earlier Wizz flight on 13 February. If we end up sleeping a night in Dubai anyway, the extra day disappears.
