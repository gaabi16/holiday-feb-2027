How we cut the cost

Sharjah instead of Dubai Air Arabia is cheap precisely because it flies from Sharjah, not Dubai. The difference is paid back in the transfer and the night of accommodation in the Gulf.

Worth knowing

  • Two airport-to-airport transfers, both in the Gulf. Outbound Dubai → Sharjah, ≈25 km. On the way back Dubai → Abu Dhabi, ≈130 km. Both on our own, with the bags in tow.
  • The return transfer is the tight one: we land in Dubai at 15:55 and Wizz leaves Abu Dhabi at 21:00, with check-in closing an hour before.
  • Two nights of accommodation — one in the Gulf and one in Larnaca, neither included in the prices above.
  • The Muscat layover, 2h 35m, is in the same Kiwi booking, so there they rebook us if we are delayed.
  • Every other ticket is separate: Wizz, Kiwi, Wizz, Wizz. None of them covers a delay on another.
  • 13 nights in Phuket, from Sunday the 14th to Saturday 27 February.
